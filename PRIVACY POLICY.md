Privacy Policy for Hey Camera

Effective date: 26 July 2026

Hey Camera respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, what is stored on your device, and the choices available to you.

# 1. Information and permissions the app uses

Hey Camera may request access to the following features and data on your device:

- Camera: Used to take photos and record videos.

- Microphone: Used to listen for voice commands.

- Photos and videos: Used to save captured media, browse your gallery, generate subtitles, extract text from pictures, and search your media.

- Notifications: Used to show progress while gallery scanning is running.

- Local device storage and app storage: Used to store app settings, downloaded speech recognition model files, generated subtitles, extracted text, object detections, and local search data.

- Internet access: Used to download required speech recognition model files or related app resources.

The app requests these permissions only when they are needed for app features.

# 2. How the app uses this data

Hey Camera uses device access to provide app features, including:

• Taking photos
• Recording videos
• Recognising voice commands
• Saving media to your device
• Browsing photos and videos
• Generating subtitles for videos
• Extracting text from pictures
• Finding people or objects in photos and videos
• Searching photos and videos
• Sharing or deleting media when you choose
• Downloading and storing speech recognition model files
• Showing scan progress while gallery scanning is running

# 3. Voice commands and microphone use

Hey Camera uses microphone access to listen for voice commands.

The app is designed to use on-device speech recognition after the required model files are available on your device. Voice input is used to recognise commands and control app features.

We do not sell your voice data.

We do not share your voice input with third parties except when required for functionality you directly request or when required by law.

You can turn voice commands on or off in the app settings.

# 4. Photos and videos

Photos and videos taken with Hey Camera are stored on your device and in your device gallery, depending on your device settings and permissions.

Hey Camera may display, share, or delete photos and videos only when you choose to use those features.

The app may also access photos and videos in your gallery so you can browse, search, subtitle, or extract text from them.

# 5. Gallery scanning

Hey Camera includes an optional gallery scanning feature.

When gallery scanning is turned on, the app may analyse photos and videos on your device so you can search for them later.

Gallery scanning may create local data such as:

• Text found in pictures
• Subtitles generated from videos
• Detected people or objects
• Local search data
• Scan status and app settings

This data is used to help you search and use your gallery inside the app.

Gallery scanning can be turned on, paused, resumed, or turned off in the app settings.

# 6. Text extraction from pictures

Hey Camera can extract text from pictures when you choose to use this feature or when gallery scanning is enabled.

The extracted text may be saved locally on your device so the app can show it again or help you search for it later.

# 7. Video subtitles

Hey Camera can generate subtitles for videos when you choose to use this feature or when gallery scanning is enabled.

To create subtitles, the app may process video audio on your device. Generated subtitle files may be saved locally on your device.

Subtitles are used to let you view captions and search for words spoken in videos.

# 8. Object and people detection

Hey Camera can detect people and objects in photos and videos when gallery scanning is enabled.

This helps you search for things such as photos with people, videos with objects, or media containing a certain type of item.

Detected objects and people may be saved as local scan data on your device.

# 9. Data sharing

Hey Camera does not sell your personal data.

Hey Camera does not sell your photos, videos, voice input, extracted text, generated subtitles, or gallery scan data.

We do not share your photos, videos, voice input, extracted text, generated subtitles, or gallery scan data with third parties except:

• When you choose to share media or content
• When required for functionality you directly request
• When required by law

# 10. Internet access and model downloads

Hey Camera may use internet access to download required speech recognition model files or related app resources.

After setup, core speech recognition is designed to work on device.

When downloading model files or app resources, your device may connect to external hosting services. Those services may receive standard connection information, such as your IP address, as part of normal internet communication.

# 11. Local storage

Hey Camera stores certain data locally on your device, such as:

• App preferences
• Permission-related settings
• Downloaded speech recognition model files
• Photos and videos saved by you
• Generated subtitles
• Extracted text from pictures
• Object and people detections
• Local gallery search data

This local data helps the app provide voice control, gallery browsing, subtitles, text extraction, and search features.

# 12. Clearing scanned gallery data

You can clear scanned gallery data in the app settings.

Clearing scanned gallery data may delete generated text, generated subtitles, object detections, and local search data.

Clearing scanned gallery data does not delete your original photos or videos.

# 13. Your choices

You can:

• Deny permissions, although some features may not work
• Turn voice commands on or off
• Turn gallery scanning on or off
• Pause or resume gallery scanning
• Clear scanned gallery data
• Share media only when you choose
• Delete media only when you choose
• Remove the app at any time
• Clear app storage in your device settings

Clearing app storage may remove app preferences, downloaded model files, and locally generated app data.

# 14. Children’s privacy

Hey Camera is not directed to children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

# 15. Security

We take reasonable steps to limit data use to what is needed for the app to function.

Most app data is stored locally on your device. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

# 16. Third-party services

Hey Camera may rely on third-party libraries, operating system services, app store services, or model hosting services to provide app functionality.

These services may process limited technical information as part of normal device, app store, or internet operation.

This Privacy Policy explains how Hey Camera uses data inside the app. It does not control the privacy practices of your device manufacturer, app store, operating system provider, internet provider, or external websites.

# 17. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this policy.
